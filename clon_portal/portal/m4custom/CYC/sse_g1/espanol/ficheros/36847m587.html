MISIÓN

Group Control performs a wide range of controlling activities such as management reporting, planning & budgeting, cost control, reporting and transfer pricing and operates in an international and dynamic environment. Next to the daily activities, Group Control participates in all finance and control related projects of the company. This gives you the opportunity to get an overview of all aspects of the company and the chance to work with a variety of people.

FUNCIONES Y ACTIVIDADES BÁSICAS

  • Support the monthly financial closing process.

  • Analysis of Atradius Profit & Loss accounts and other management.

  • Produce periodic management information and respond to periodic/ad hoc information request.

  • Maintain the CXO management dashboard tool (management information tool).

  • Perform the role of account manager for assigned Organisation Units.
