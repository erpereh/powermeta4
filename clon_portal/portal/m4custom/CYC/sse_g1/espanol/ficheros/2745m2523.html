Nombre de puestoCompliance Analyst
MISIÓN

The mission of Atradius’ Compliance Function is to support and advise Atradius and its management in achieving its mission statement through compliance practices, which contribute to sound and controlled business conduct and practices in every aspect of Atradius’ organization, and jointly with the business fully integrate Compliance Risk management in all daily business activities of Atradius.

FUNCIONES Y ACTIVIDADES BÁSICAS

• Support the operational aspects of the sanctions checking and customer/counterparty due diligence (CDD) process in general, mainly focused on the centralised tasks in the sanctions and CDD checking process.

• Support the assessment of alerts identified in the sanctions checking tools (including LexisNexis and the internal systems), mainly focused on false positive selections, registration in the systems and internal communication.

• Support the sanctions screening process in the assessment of alerts identified as true matches, provide assistance to local compliance and other internal stakeholders on sanctions related issues, and participate in the delivery of sanctions training to employees.

• Proactively contribute to enhance compliance policies and procedures to continually improve internal controls to comply with laws and regulations.

• Provide compliance input to projects with a compliance component being implemented within the organization.

• Liaise with the Representatives of the Local Compliance Function in the countries where Atradius operate and provide assistance as required.

• Monitor industry developments and maintain knowledge of new and amended laws and regulations that may impact Atradius’ operations.

• Provide compliance advice in matters arising in other Business Units in the regular conduct of business activities within the organisation.

• Assist in the coordination of responses to requests received by Regulatory Authorities, gathering the information required from different Business Units within the organization.
