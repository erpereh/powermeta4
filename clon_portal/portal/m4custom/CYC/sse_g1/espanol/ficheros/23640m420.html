Nombre de puestoCompliance Coordinator
MISIÓN

The Compliance Coordinator responsibilities cover a wide range of tasks, successfully contributing to the Group Compliance Function providing assistance to Atradius’ activities globally. The GCC will assist in the design, implementation and analysis of the compliance program of the organization, assist in the preparation of regular reports for Atradius’ Management, provide advice to other Business Units on compliance matters, participate in the coordination of responses to queries received from Regulatory Authorities worldwide and provide assistance in Data Protection matters.

FUNCIONES Y ACTIVIDADES BÁSICAS

• Proactively contribute to enhance compliance policies and procedures to continually improve internal controls to comply with laws and regulations.

• Provide compliance input to projects with a compliance component being implemented within the organization.

• Liaise with the Representatives of the Local Compliance Function in the countries where Atradius operate and provide assistance as required.

• Provide input and support in the drafting of compliance policies and procedures and presentations and reports for Atradius Management.

• Monitor industry developments and maintain knowledge of new and amended laws and regulations that may impact Atradius’ operations.

• Provide compliance advice in matters arising in other Business Units in the regular conduct of business activities within the organisation.

• Assist in the coordination of responses to requests received by Regulatory Authorities, gathering the information required from different Business Units within the organization.

• Assist the Group Compliance Manager and Group Data Protection Officer in Data Protection matters in his obligations to monitor adherence to Data Protection legislation and internal procedures.
