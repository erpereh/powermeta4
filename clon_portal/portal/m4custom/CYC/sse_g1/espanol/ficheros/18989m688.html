MISIÓN

Group Control performs a wide range of controlling activities such as management reporting, planning & budgeting, cost control and transfer pricing and a projects unit and operates in an international and dynamic environment. Next to the daily activities, Group Control participates in all finance and control related projects of the company. This gives you the opportunity to get an overview of all aspects of the company and the chance to work with a variety of people.

FUNCIONES Y ACTIVIDADES BÁSICAS

• Support the manager of the Projects unit in managing the IFRS 17 Workstreams Business change Management of the Group Control unit. The responsibility of the Business Change Management workstream is to drive the change within the organisation, which includes amongst others to assess the impact on routines, process and tools, around steering, reporting and planning and forecasting, to analyse existing KPI and propose alternatives. And to communicate towards stakeholders and execute training plans. This large project will be a dominant activity in the coming years.

• The projects unit coordinates technical subjects within Group Control which involves to contribute to represent Group Control in committees such as calculation of Cost of Capital, required Return rates, etc,

• Account management: Within Group Control account managers are appointed as contact point for, and knowledge expert on, one or more dedicated business units or support functions. In monthly meetings with local controllers, the account manager’s knowledge base should be extended and a close relationship with the unit should be build. The Financial analyst in the Projects unit will also perform the role of account manager for one or more units.

• Perform the control function for a designated Support Function Unit. Group Control performs the control function for certain smaller support function units (e.g. Internal Audit, Corporate Center). These units are assigned to individual team members of Group Control, potentially from the Projects team.
